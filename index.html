About Us

Building enduring value through strategic partnerships

Bridgeway Capital is a distinguished private equity firm focused on control-oriented investments in middle-market companies across North America. We partner with founders and management teams to accelerate growth, drive operational excellence, and build lasting enterprises.

Our approach combines deep industry expertise, rigorous analytical frameworks, and a genuine commitment to the companies we back. We invest with a long-term horizon, typically holding our investments for 5–10 years, allowing us to focus on sustainable value creation rather than short-term optimization.
