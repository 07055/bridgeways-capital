About Us

Bridgeway Capital is a private equity firm focused on middle-market investments. We partner with management teams to build stronger, more valuable companies through strategic guidance and operational support.

What is Private Equity?

Private equity is investment capital that is not listed on a public exchange. Firms like Bridgeway Capital raise funds from institutional investors — such as pension funds, endowments, and family offices — to acquire ownership stakes in private companies. We typically take a controlling interest, allowing us to work closely with management to improve operations, accelerate growth, and increase long-term value.

Our Approach

We target established businesses with strong fundamentals, clear competitive advantages, and significant room for growth. Our team conducts deep due diligence across financial, operational, and commercial dimensions before making an investment. Post-acquisition, we partner with management to execute a shared value-creation plan — investing in talent, technology, new markets, and strategic acquisitions.

Why Partner With Us

With a long-term investment horizon (typically 5–10 years), we are not driven by short-term market pressures. Our interests are fully aligned with our investors and management teams through meaningful equity co-investment. We bring more than capital — we bring operational expertise, industry relationships, and a proven track record of building market-leading companies.
